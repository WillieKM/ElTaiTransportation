About El Tai Non Emergency Medical Transport Services

Compassionate non-emergency medical transportation for patients and families across Snohomish County, King County, Pierce County and Thurston County Washington.

4 Counties Served
100% Wheelchair Accessible
7 Days Available Daily

El Tai Transport Services provides reliable and compassionate non-emergency medical transportation throughout Snohomish County, King County, Pierce County and Thurston County Washington.

Our wheelchair accessible vehicles allow passengers with mobility challenges to travel safely to and from medical appointments, with professional drivers trained to assist every step of the way.
